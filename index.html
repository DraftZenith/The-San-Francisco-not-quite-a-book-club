Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect before you decide.

If something is not answered here, email us. We would rather clarify the details before you commit.

Is the Author Spotlight free?

No. The packages shown on this page are paid services. Please review the price and deliverables for the package you are considering before agreeing to proceed.

Do I need to pay before a preliminary Zoom call?

We do not currently offer preliminary one-to-one Zoom calls as a standard step in the process. Please email your questions first. We will clarify the service and its written scope so you can decide whether to proceed before paying.

What happens after I contact you?

We will respond by email, clarify your goals and any questions, confirm the package scope and timing in writing, and explain the next steps. Do not make a payment until you are comfortable with the written details.

Will you guarantee book sales, reviews, or attendance?

No. We can describe the promotional work and placements included in a package, but we cannot guarantee purchases, reviews, recommendations, audience size, or sales results.

Can I ask questions before paying?

Yes. Email us with any questions about deliverables, timing, payment, cancellation, or how the Spotlight works. We encourage authors to understand the terms before making a financial commitment.

Can I choose a package and schedule later?

Scheduling depends on availability. The intended timing and deliverables should be confirmed in writing before payment, and any changes should be agreed by email.

Will my book be reviewed or endorsed?

A Spotlight is a promotional/editorial feature, not a promise of an independent review or endorsement. Any reader opinions are their own and should be represented accurately.

What if I have a concern about the service?

Contact us by email before paying. Ask for clarification of the scope, delivery schedule, cancellation terms, and refund policy. These terms should be confirmed in writing before the collaboration begins.
